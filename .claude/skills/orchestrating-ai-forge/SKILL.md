# Orchestrate AI Forge

## Establish truth

1. Resolve the real working directory.
2. Read applicable instructions and repository state.
3. Separate confirmed facts, volatile claims, personal judgments and unknowns.
4. Detect privacy, publication and credential boundaries before expanding scope.

## Create one mission

Define:

- one outcome;
- one active project;
- one owner or operator;
- at most three executable steps;
- one acceptance gate;
- one fallback;
- one next physical action under 30 minutes.

Keep other ideas in `NEXT`, `INCUBATING` or `ARCHIVE`. Do not let them enter
active execution.

## Route work

Choose operators by responsibility:

- discovery and evidence;
- architecture and implementation;
- review and adversarial critique;
- testing and release.

Do not assign two operators the same responsibility without an explicit
independent-review reason.

## Preserve the truth envelope

Attach these fields to changing claims:

`source_type`, `verification_status`, `last_checked_at`, `confidence`, `owner`.

Read [truth-and-handoff.md](references/truth-and-handoff.md) before creating a
cross-agent handoff or a publication plan.

## Close the loop

Require:

1. diff or artifact evidence;
2. relevant checks;
3. audit event;
4. unresolved risks;
5. next owner and next action.

Do not mark a mission complete because a plan or mockup exists.
