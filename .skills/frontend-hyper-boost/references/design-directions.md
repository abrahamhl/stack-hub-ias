# Design directions

Choose one primary direction and one counterpoint. Do not blend every reference.

## REQUIRED for AI Forge product surfaces

**Primary direction:** Fallout shelter factory  
**Execution level:** PREMIUM (21st.dev / Behance), never “admin template”.  
**Grammar library:** `premium-21st-registry.md` (17 anclajes mandatorios de
Abraham: shader cards, hero futuristic, sphere/swirl de proceso, partículas de
flujo, orrey, galerías orbitales, barras, círculos de fase).

**Counterpoint under the factory:** Claude-code editorial — only for config,
protocol text, long reading, and legal. The Factory Floor and agent rooms must
feel like a live simulator (depth, orbs, lanes, particles), not a wiki.

**Hard rule:** If the UI could be mistaken for a generic SaaS settings page,
it fails the AI Forge bar. Re-read `premium-21st-registry.md` and raise craft.

## Retrocyber terminal

- Dark mineral surfaces, terminal typography, vector grids, scanline restraint.
- ASCII relief, pixel icons, exposed system states, phosphor accents.
- Counterpoint: warm paper, editorial serif, or clean utility white.

## Fallout shelter factory

- Rooms, inhabitants, production lanes, meters, physical switches, audit boards.
- Use spatial metaphor to explain ownership and flow.
- Never let the metaphor hide basic navigation, filters, or state.

## Cyberpunk layered city

- Foreground signage, midground interface, background environment.
- Depth, cutouts, holographic panels, volumetric light, dense typography.
- Reserve maximum density for story zones; keep task zones calm.

## Claude-code editorial

- Quiet dark shell, precise mono details, roomy content, strong text hierarchy.
- Use this as the operational base under louder creative scenes.

## Y2K techno poster

- Oversized type, chrome/plastic relief, acid accents, deliberate collisions.
- Preserve line length, contrast, and a stable CTA plane.

## Psytrance main stage

- Symmetry, sacred geometry, reactive particles, audio-linked layers, avatars.
- Always require sound consent and a non-audio state.

## Cartoon world-change

- A focused object can recompose the whole palette, camera, depth, and character
  pose.
- Keep the change reversible and explain the target before activation.
